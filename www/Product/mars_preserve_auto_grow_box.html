DATA / resource efficiency

More control over every drop.

Hydroponics delivers nutrients directly to plant roots, while sensors and environmental controls help the grower tune conditions to the crop. The system is designed to make inputs visible, measurable and easier to manage.

◉

Vertical growing. Smaller footprint.

Multi-level grow racks use the container's interior volume to support dense, organized production. Lighting, irrigation and airflow can be managed as part of one integrated environment.
