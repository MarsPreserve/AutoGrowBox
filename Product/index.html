Inside the Auto Grow Box

A growing environment, built into a container.

The concept combines vertical plant racks, nutrient-water circulation, grow lighting, and monitoring equipment within a modular shipping-container footprint.

  • ✓Water-based growing: nutrient solution is delivered to plants through a hydroponic system.
  • ✓Sensor-supported oversight: collect information about conditions and help operators make adjustments.
  • ✓Controlled environment: lighting and climate systems support a managed indoor growing space.
  • ✓Modular footprint: a container format can be considered for a range of deployment locations.

Questions, answered.

Explore the core ideas behind the Mars Preserve Auto Grow Box concept.

What is the Mars Preserve Auto Grow Box?

A concept for a container-based indoor growing system that combines hydroponics and agritechnology to support fresh produce production.

What does hydroponics mean?

Hydroponics is a method of growing plants using a nutrient-rich water solution rather than conventional soil. System design and crop requirements determine how it is operated.

What role do AI and sensors play?

Sensors can monitor relevant growing conditions. Software can help organize readings, surface changes, and support operator decisions. Specific automation features depend on the implemented hardware and software.

Can it grow food in any location?

The container format is intended to support flexible placement, but a real deployment still needs suitable power, water, site access, environmental management, and local compliance.
