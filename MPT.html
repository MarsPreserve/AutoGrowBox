The Technology

Agriculture becomes infrastructure.

Instead of relying entirely on outdoor conditions, controlled environments create a repeatable space where the variables affecting plant growth can be monitored and managed.

💧

Hydroponic Growing

Deliver water and nutrients directly to plants while reducing dependence on conventional soil-based cultivation.

🌡️

Climate Control

Monitor and regulate temperature, humidity, airflow and other environmental variables inside an enclosed growing space.

💡

Adaptive Lighting

Controlled lighting gives crops a predictable photoperiod and allows the growing environment to operate independently of outdoor daylight.

🤖

Automation

Sensors and control systems can automate irrigation, lighting, nutrient delivery and environmental responses.

📦

Modular Containers

Shipping-container infrastructure provides a rugged, transportable enclosure for building scalable controlled growing environments.

🌱

Microgreens & Veggies

Create an environment suited to high-value crops including microgreens, herbs, leafy greens and vegetables.

AutoGrowBox Concept

A farm that fits inside the box.

The AutoGrowBox concept combines compact infrastructure, environmental sensing and automation into a controlled cultivation platform. The larger vision extends that philosophy into modular shipping-container agriculture.

  • ✓ Automated irrigation and nutrient delivery
  • ✓ Environmental sensing and monitoring
  • ✓ Adjustable lighting and climate controls
  • ✓ Modular architecture for expansion
  • ✓ Designed around accessible, maintainable components

Project Roadmap

From prototype to platform.

Mars Preserve's public roadmap describes a progression from digital infrastructure and research toward physical AutoGrowBox development and modular expansion.
